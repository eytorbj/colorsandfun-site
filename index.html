Questions & answers

What do I get, and in what format?

You get printable PDF files, delivered as an instant digital download right after checkout. Nothing is shipped. The PDFs open on any computer, tablet or phone and print on a regular home printer.

Which paper size should I use?

Both! Every book includes a US Letter (8.5 × 11 in) PDF and an A4 (210 × 297 mm) PDF, so use whichever matches the paper in your printer.

Can I print it more than once? Can I use it in my classroom?

Yes. Print as many copies as you like for your own family or your own classroom. Please don't resell or share the PDF files themselves. If you'd like to use the pages more widely (for example across a whole school), just email us.

What is your refund policy?

Because these are digital downloads that you receive instantly, purchases are generally non-refundable. If anything is wrong with your files, for example a file won't open or a page is missing, email us and we'll make it right.

Is there a version in Icelandic?

Yes! Both books are also available in Icelandic. See the Íslenska section below.

How can I contact you?

Email us at colorsandfun@colorsandfun.com. We're happy to help with downloads, printing or anything else.
